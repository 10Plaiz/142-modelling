### Conditional recommendation

At estimated normal demand, average total delay was 2.57 seconds for A, 1.80 for B, and 1.70 for C. B saved 0.77 seconds per car for 2 additional scheduled guard-hours. C saved 0.87 seconds for 3 additional guard-hours. Extending B to C saved 0.10 seconds; its paired 95% interval for C minus B was [-0.13, -0.08] seconds.

Across 80%, 100%, and 120% demand, the largest mean delay was 3.21 seconds. No spillover was observed in the nine study combinations. This does not prove zero risk. Under these estimates, retain A as the starting staffing choice: the experiment has not established a need for more guards. Additional staffing has a measured time benefit, but its financial value was not modelled. This recommendation applies to the assumed arrangement and inputs. It is not a verified staffing recommendation for the real campus.
